            How to use this desk

            A practical guide to finding evidence, checking property records, and knowing when information leaves this machine.

            Local-first guide

            Purpose

            Evidence-backed NYC housing research, in one private workspace.

            Use this site to search installed housing-law sources, inspect the passages behind a result, and look up bounded NYC HPD violation records. It is designed for research you can verify—not for replacing an attorney or treating a generated answer as legal authority.

            Recommended workflow

            From question to verified source

            Each step keeps the evidence and its boundaries visible.

            1. 01

              Prepare the source library

              Install the core publications once, then update them periodically. Source search is free and local after installation.

            2. 02

              Ask one specific question

              Use a citation, legal topic, or plain-language question. For a building, enter its address and confirm the intended HPD record.

            3. 03

              Choose the right research mode

              Search locally for source text, use a provider only when you want a generated explanation, or choose property lookup for HPD records.

            4. 04

              Read past the answer

              Inspect cited excerpts, open the official publication, and check that the law is current before relying on a result.

            Reading results

            Three checks before you rely on anything

            1. CitationDoes the section or record actually match your question?
            2. ContextRead the full excerpt and nearby provisions, not only the summary.
            3. CurrencyOpen the official source and confirm the law or record is current.

            Before you act

            Know the boundary

            • Local

              Installed-source search and workspace records stay on this machine.

            • Network

              Source updates and property lookup contact official external services.

            • May charge

              Generated answers, summaries, and semantic indexing can use your provider key. Review estimates and limits first.

            Be concrete
